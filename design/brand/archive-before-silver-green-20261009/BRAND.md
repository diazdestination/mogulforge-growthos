# MogulForge mark and GrowthOS visual direction

The owner approved a **similar original mark** on September 30, 2026 because the
source poster's M was generated by AI and no editable master exists. The source
poster remains at `../agent-team-reference.jpg`.

## Canonical asset

`../../artifacts/command-center/public/brand/mogulforge-mark.svg` is the clean,
scalable platform mark. It is a geometric angular M with an inner chevron,
inspired by the poster's top-left emblem but drawn as new vector paths. It
replaces the first raster draft. The transparent mark is intended for dark
platform surfaces; do not place it over a busy photo or use it as a client's
white-label identity. The generated exploration is retained as
`mogulforge-mark-ai-concept.png`, not served by the product.

Primary colors in the agent-team surface: ink `#05111E`, panel `#0B1B2B`,
stroke `#294156`, warm gold `#E9AD40`, light gold `#FFD57A`, and text `#F5F7FA`.
Keep the flat gold mark at least 24 CSS pixels tall and preserve clear space
around it. Wordmarks are real text in the application for legibility.

## Generation and approval boundary

Built-in image generation produced the original transparent concept using the
owner's poster as a style reference. Prompt: “Create one original, clean
geometric M monogram: two tall angular outer wings that converge into an inner
chevron, with balanced negative space and a subtle forged-shield feeling;
strong at 24 pixels, flat warm gold, transparent background, no text, no
gradient, no mockup.” Its raster artifacts were removed by redrawing the
geometry as SVG. The vector is now the candidate brand asset authorized by
the owner to use in GrowthOS; no trademark search or registration is implied.

## Usage boundary

The public GrowthOS landing page, agent-team page, and browser favicon use the
platform mark. The authenticated shell still shows the actual tenant's name
and initial, preserving contractor white-label separation. Do not propagate
the MogulForge mark into contractor Smart Sites, sender identities, customer
messages, or documents without that client's branding decision.
