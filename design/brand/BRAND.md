# MogulForge original silver and green identity

> **Current context — October 29, 2026:** This approved identity is retained in current GrowthOS source. The original branch and publication checkpoint below describe the October 9 restoration, not the current branch or deployed state. Branding does not establish provider acceptance.
> See [current build](../../CURRENT-BUILD.md) and [documentation map](../../docs/DOCUMENTATION-MAP.md).


On October 9, 2026 the owner selected the earlier silver shield M with green growth bars from their August 20 ad artwork, superseding the September 30 gold angular-M candidate. Scope: MogulForge marketing website and GrowthOS product. Local source restoration only; publication is separate.

The original high-resolution assets remain in the marketing repository public/assets/brand. The product retains byte-identical copies as public/brand/mogulforge-logo-original.png and mogulforge-logo-original-dark.jpeg. The served mogulforge-mark.svg uses an SVG viewport onto the byte-identical original JPEG, preserving the actual metallic artwork. It is not a new editable vector master.

Palette: ink #05111E, panels #0B1B2B, signal green #BAF000, highlight #D6F877, silver #D7DFE5, text #F5F7FA. Light-mode primary uses deep green #4D7000 for readable white labels; dark-mode controls use bright green with dark labels. Operational warning/error colors retain their meanings.

Landing, agent team, favicon and MogulForge sign-in use the restored mark. The authenticated shell still displays the tenant's own business name and initial. No client sites, communications identities, data, providers or tenant records are rebranded.

Pre-change files, including the gold mark, are preserved verbatim under archive-before-silver-green-20261009 and in Git history. Marketing and product source live on codex/silver-green-brand-20261009.
