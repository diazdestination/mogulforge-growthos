# GrowthOS contractor product and subscription direction

> **Current context — October 29, 2026:** Read the current source inventory before interpreting old product/packaging stages. Core contractor operations, saved scheduling, service and communication foundations are implemented; prices, entitlements and future add-ons are not live offers or proven acceptance. Full contractor replacement remains the requirement.
> See [current build](CURRENT-BUILD.md) and [documentation map](docs/DOCUMENTATION-MAP.md).


Owner direction: October 1, 2026. Working product specification, not a published
offer, purchase, customer contract, entitlement migration or production release.

## Decision

**Latest owner clarification:** GrowthOS should replace Housecall Pro for the
beta companies, with simpler navigation and dependable native scheduling,
invoices and warranty/service tracking. Read `GROWTHOS-CONTRACTOR-OPERATIONS.md`.
The earlier add-on positioning below is historical context. Housecall Pro
connectivity is optional migration/transition support; native operation must
not require a continuing Housecall Pro subscription. Replacement readiness
requires accepted daily workflows and reconciled migration, not more features
alone. Existing beta agreements and live release gates remain unchanged.

Build one configurable contractor growth platform with a flagship package worth
at least **$1,500 per month**. Two companies, roofing and HVAC, have agreed to a
special **$500/month limited beta** according to Adam. Neither activation nor
collection has been verified. The beta does not include every feature. Honor
anything already promised and agree the included scope before activation; do not
retroactively remove access or invent an expiry date.

Adam reports "House pro", provisionally interpreted as Housecall Pro. Which
company uses it and their plan eligibility remain to be confirmed. Both Smart
Sites and existing websites must be supported. A Smart Site is optional.

This replaces the earlier immediate $2,500 managed-estimate-recovery cohort
recommendation. It authorizes scoped local product improvement. The existing
paid-beta release gates still govern live activation. Do not reopen speculative
platform expansion just to increase a feature count.

## Customer message

**Your contractor workday, clearly organized.**

Schedule work, prepare invoices, and keep warranty and service follow-ups in one
workspace. Connect a MogulForge Smart Site or your existing website, then add
the tested growth automations your team needs. The daily path is Today,
Customers & Jobs, Schedule, Invoices, and Warranties & Service.

The native-contractor-day candidate implements internal invoice issue/void,
recorded receipt reconciliation and explicit service recurrence. Email delivery,
customer payment collection and migrated opening balances require separate
acceptance. Use this message only alongside the capabilities actually enabled.

Use the headline as positioning, not a guarantee. Before selling, distinguish
implemented, configured, tested and live capabilities. Describe Housecall Pro
connectivity as planned until the actual connector passes acceptance. A lead
receipt is not a booking, provider delivery is not a reply, and a recorded win
is not collected cash or incremental profit.

### Why switch, and why pay a premium?

The beta buyers described confusing navigation and unreliable daily work.
Replacing that pain requires observable usability and dependable handoffs.
"AI follow-up" alone does not establish a premium offer. Validate the daily
replacement first, then show the value of accountable response, source-aware
recovery, service retention and a bounded optimization service. Do not require a
second permanent Housecall Pro subscription as a condition of native operation.

## Package design

Prices beyond the agreed beta are target packaging hypotheses; validate delivery
cost and buyer value before publishing. No Stripe prices were created.

| Package | Price direction | Included scope to validate | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founding beta | $500/month, already agreed | One business/location, one website connection, one selected trade playbook, core lead inbox and ownership, one approved follow-up workflow, basic outcome review | Define active opportunity/contact allowance and service hours after initial data review; no unlimited custom integrations, voice or campaign services |
| Growth | $1,500/month flagship target | One location, website + one supported business-system connector, response ownership/escalation, estimate recovery, reply/task triage, customer history, outcome report and a bounded monthly optimization review | Candidate ceiling: three active workflows; actual volume, channel allowance and service hours must be specified in the offer |
| Scale | $2,500–$3,000/month target | Growth plus repeat-business/maintenance workflows, expanded campaign and source reporting, additional team workflows, higher capacity and support allowance | Additional locations, bespoke work and provider use explicitly priced; only sell tested modules |
| Multi-location | $5,000+/month scoped target | Location oversight, separate client/location permissions, larger workflow capacity and agreed service coverage | Requires proven multi-location operations and support economics; not an enterprise-readiness claim |

Potential add-ons: Smart Site build/hosting/content service; approved voice or
missed-call recovery; approved SMS capacity; additional locations; additional
connector installations; content/SEO operations. Each needs a defined scope,
usage allowance, operating cost and release evidence. Never charge separately
for a feature already included in a customer's agreement. Provider/AI costs are
included within an explicit allowance or separately disclosed; no surprise
automatic overage charges.

Every tier retains tenant security, permissions, contact suppression, delivery
failure visibility, audit history, basic support/recovery and usable data export
when lifecycle delivery is completed. These are baseline trust requirements.

## What makes the subscription valuable to keep

- A reliable daily queue: what needs attention, why, who owns it and what happens next.
- Customer/property/opportunity history that reduces repeated work.
- Working website and business-system connections with visible freshness and failures.
- Trade-specific playbooks tuned to the company's services, geography and capacity.
- A recurring owner review showing activity, conversions, collections and unknowns honestly.
- Better decisions from accumulated, permissioned outcome history.

Retention must come from recurring usefulness. Provide clear cancellation,
export/offboarding, ownership and data-retention rules. Do not trap the customer
through missing exports, withheld credentials or deliberately difficult cancellation.

## Product experience

Contractor-facing destinations: **Today**, **Customers & Jobs**, **Schedule**,
**Invoices**, and **Warranties & Service**. The native-contractor-day candidate
implements that organization using existing customer/job routes, a read-only
daily snapshot, and shared role-aware navigation/search. Revenue, growth,
reports and administration remain secondary destinations. GrowthOS billing is
labelled Subscription to distinguish it from customer invoices.

- Default to today's actionable work with owner, reason, last real interaction,
  source freshness and a clear next action. Hide developer configuration by default.
- Actions say what they do: review message, assign owner, log outcome, reconcile failure.
- Clearly distinguish needs review, scheduled, executed, failed and uncertain.
- Show empty/error/stale states rather than invented zeros or false success.
- Preserve drafts on refresh/failure; prevent duplicate consequential actions.
- Mobile acceptance at 390px and narrow 320px; keyboard and accessible names.
- Guided setup: business/trade -> source/website -> responder/permissions ->
  contact policy -> test evidence -> explicit activation. Never fake completed steps.
- In pilot observation, require an office responder to find the next action,
  handle a reply and record an outcome without coaching. Proposed usability
  target: each common task within two minutes; measure before claiming success.

## One core with configurable trade packs

Shared core: tenant identity, contact/property/opportunity graph, source mappings,
inbox, permissions, execution ledger, suppression, scheduling, audit, billing
entitlements, reporting and lifecycle controls. Trade packs configure vocabulary,
services, qualification questions, templates, stages and workflow policies.
Customer variations belong in versioned configuration, not repository forks.

| Roofing pack | HVAC pack | First useful release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repair/replacement inquiry qualification | Repair/replacement/maintenance qualification | Existing intake services plus approved company questions |
| Inspection request and office confirmation | Service request and urgent office escalation | Requests only until calendars/capacity can be checked; no guaranteed dispatch |
| Unsold repair/replacement estimate follow-up | Unsold replacement estimate follow-up | Source history and current status required; this is a priority |
| Photo/document/customer room handoff | Equipment/service history and customer handoff | Reuse existing evidence/rooms; equipment fields only when justified by the workflow |
| Warranty/check-in and referral workflow | Maintenance renewal and seasonal service workflow | Later bounded workflow pack; validate eligibility and contact permissions |

Do not generate insurance coverage decisions, financing promises, technical safety
diagnoses or installation/dispatch guarantees from model guesses. Company-approved
content and a named office responder govern customer-facing decisions.

## Integration architecture

```text
Smart Site OR existing website form
  -> supported site backend / secure relay
  -> existing tenant-scoped GrowthOS intake + receipt
  -> shared opportunity record + owner + follow-up task

Housecall Pro (eligible access) OR approved exports
  -> connector adapter + original IDs/dates + freshness
  -> same opportunity model, reconciliation and history

Shared model -> Revenue Desk -> governed action -> receipt -> actual outcome
```

Housecall Pro remains authoritative for its jobs, estimates, appointments and
invoices unless an explicit field-ownership rule says otherwise. GrowthOS owns
its workflow recommendations, approvals and execution history. Do not require
duplicate data entry or let two automation systems contact the same opportunity
without coordination. Initial integration should be read-only; introduce writes
only with endpoint, role, conflict, idempotency and rollback/reconciliation proof.

### Existing websites

The current Smart Site intake protocol is website-independent and server-only.
Reuse it for an existing website after adapter installation and acceptance.
No site replacement or DNS transfer is required by the architecture. A static
site may need a supported form service/backend or a secure relay. Compatibility
with individual WordPress/Wix/Squarespace/Webflow plugins is not verified.

Reuse per-site credentials, request IDs, consent receipts, staff assignment,
launch guard and saved lead/receipt semantics. Private server keys never go in
browser snippets. Preserve the stable request ID and original normalized payload
for a retry; changed payload with the same ID is a conflict. A network timeout
does not prove the inquiry was not saved. Durable encrypted site-side recovery,
alerts and reconciliation must be verified per installation.

Legacy mapped capture endpoints exist separately and are not equivalent to the
guarded intake protocol. Audit their consent, deduplication, origin/spam controls,
recovery and activation behavior before recommending them for a new paid pilot.
An existing website connection is not a CRM sync.

### Housecall Pro

Official API Overview, checked October 1, says API access is on MAX, and admins
can generate read-only or full-access keys. Verify the customer's actual account
and endpoint coverage before promising sync or asking them to buy an upgrade.
No account was accessed and no API capability was tested in this assessment.

If access is unavailable, use customer-approved exports for baseline/assisted
operation, label their freshness, and require current human verification before
outreach. A CSV snapshot cannot safely stand in for live replies, opt-outs or
current job status. Continue website intake independently.

Mapping must preserve external customer/opportunity/estimate IDs, original dates,
status timestamps, last real contact, amounts/currency, source and ingestion
timestamps separately. Reimports update by external opportunity identity; a
repeat job is not a duplicate person. Unknown values remain unknown. Quarantine
ambiguous mappings. Late events must not rewind current state.

## Tier enforcement design: required, not implemented yet

Current billing uses a single `STRIPE_PRICE_ID` and stores a Stripe price ID in
`organizations.planTier`. It has a global optional subscription paywall, not a
feature entitlement system. Do not pretend package copy alone enforces tiers.

Implement a versioned server-owned plan catalog with stable internal plan IDs,
explicit capabilities, limits and approved per-tenant beta grants. Resolve
Stripe price IDs through an allowlist mapping; never trust a plan sent by the
browser. Existing tenants require an explicit migration/grant review; no silent
access removal. Development/demo settings must not become a production bypass.

Check permissions AND entitlements before work is queued and immediately before
execution. Show the same capability state in the UI, with reason and upgrade
path. Enforce resource/usage caps atomically; meter provider attempts accurately
and do not charge duplicate retries as new customer usage.

Verify signed billing webhooks, deduplicate events, reconcile out-of-order events
against current subscription state, and test upgrades, scheduled downgrades,
cancellation and payment-failure policy. Preserve reads/history/export and
cancel/refund/offboarding access according to the customer's agreed terms.
Uncertain queued provider effects remain reconcilable after downgrade; never
blindly replay or erase them. All plan changes need audit events. Stripe test
mode acceptance precedes any real billing action.

## What makes $1,500 credible

Measure from the two betas: eligible opportunity volume, actual response times,
conversations, appointments, estimate wins, collected amounts, contribution where
known, staff time, service hours, usage costs, retention and willingness to pay
for the expanded package. Compare appropriate cohorts; influenced outcomes are
not automatically caused by GrowthOS. Seasonal or long-cycle work needs enough
observation to avoid misleading before/after comparisons.

Illustrative target, not a claim: if a customer seeks 3x monthly contribution
relative to a $1,500 fee, the system must support $4,500 of incremental monthly
contribution or equivalent documented operational value. One $10,000 estimate
is not $10,000 of profit. A company with insufficient volume/capacity may fit the
smaller package better; this is a qualification issue, not a reason to add AI.

Proposed internal delivery target: at least 70% contribution margin after direct
hosting/provider costs and delivery labor. At $1,500 that leaves a $450 monthly
direct-cost budget. Measure actual loaded labor and provider use before setting
allowances. Initial beta onboarding can be a separate learning investment, but
track it. Neither this target nor a price point is evidence of market fit.
