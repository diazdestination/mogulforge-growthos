# Make the first two contractor accounts successful

Working operator plan. No new customer promises, price changes or activation.

## Offer and scope

**Your contractor workday, clearly organized.** Schedule jobs, prepare invoices,
and keep customer service follow-ups in one place. Connect a Smart Site or an
existing website. Add growth workflows only after their delivery and economics
are demonstrated.

| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Painless Roofing and Water Restoration | Limited beta, $500/month      | Domain, source access, staff, lead volumes, conversion, collections and warranty agreements pending |
| Climate Simulation Solutions           | Limited beta, $500/month      | Same unknowns; HVAC Smart Site exists as a private candidate, not a live customer site              |

Preserve these agreements. Confirm what was already promised before assigning a
plan. Core security, failure visibility, customer export and clear access cannot
be premium-only benefits. Invoicing is internal document/receipt management in
the prior candidate; actual invoice delivery and opening-balance migration are
remaining acceptance work. The owner's October 2 clarification defers online
contractor payment collection to a future add-on. Staff-recorded external receipts
and balances remain in scope, but no processor choice or in-app charge is required.

## A short office-and-field rehearsal

Use an authorized synthetic job first, then approved real company configuration.
Have an office user and a field user attempt the actions without coaching.
Record task time, wrong turns and help requests. Proposed usability target:
ordinary tasks under two minutes; this is a target, not a measured claim.

| Step                              | Observable acceptance                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Find today's work                 | Correct company/date/timezone, actual owner, overdue work visible and directly openable                        |
| Schedule or reschedule            | Accepted staff only, valid start/end, conflicts explained, local time preserved, retry does not duplicate      |
| Complete the job                  | Status persists, invoice-preparation handoff appears once, reopened work retracts that prompt                  |
| Prepare and issue invoice         | Customer/scope/tax reviewed, number stable, issued contents preserved, clear distinction from sent             |
| Record an actual receipt          | Partial payment/balance correct, repeated request cannot create more cash, existing ledger receipt reused      |
| Correct an allocation             | Receipt history retained, cash unchanged, refund ambiguity visibly requires review                             |
| Complete agreed recurring service | Evidence and dates clear; exactly one next obligation/task; no invented warranty coverage or automatic booking |
| Recover from a failure            | Staff see unresolved state, can retry the same command or escalate with context; no false success              |

Do not replace the old system until the team's critical workflows, documents,
opening balances and authority/cutover decision are reconciled. Keep one writer
per record type and a tested rollback/export path. Cancelling an old subscription
is a distinct customer decision.

## Measure value before raising the offer

Gather a dated baseline from an approved source, retaining unknowns rather than
zeroes: inquiries, first human response times, qualified opportunities, booked
work, estimates sent/accepted, completed jobs, invoices issued, collections,
refunds, service obligations due/completed, staff/admin time and support time.
Compare matching periods and cohorts. Keep staff-recorded outcomes distinct
from provider-confirmed delivery, settlement and independently verified effects.

Each weekly review has three outputs: one verified improvement, one recurring
failure repaired, and one explicit next decision. Track staff adoption and
renewal intent alongside recovered opportunities. A recorded win or paid invoice
does not by itself prove incremental revenue caused by GrowthOS.

The $1,500 flagship should combine an accepted native workday, a small number of
proven growth workflows, reliable monitoring and a bounded operating review.
Record channel, AI, hosting, onboarding and support costs per company. Add-ons
may include a Smart Site, approved voice/SMS capacity or another location after
testing and explicit scope. Do not sell unlimited custom integrations or imply
that every newly coded feature has complete tier enforcement.

For scale context only, 112 retained accounts at $1,500/month equal $2.016 million
in annual subscription revenue before costs, churn and taxes. That arithmetic
is not a forecast. The next commercial milestone is two customers using the
system successfully, paying the agreed fee and choosing to renew.

## Stop expansion when these fail

- Staff still need repeated help to find or finish ordinary work.
- Missed, duplicated or uncertain financial/communication effects cannot be reconciled.
- Setup and monthly support consume the margin needed to serve more accounts.
- Buyers do not value the proven outcomes above their existing alternatives.
- Customer-specific forks or manual desktop steps grow with every account.

Repair the failing constraint before adding another module. Keep trade-specific
configuration in one product and document repeatable onboarding for the next
contractor. Current technical evidence is in the native-contractor-day reports;
real adoption, willingness to pay and profitability remain unverified.
