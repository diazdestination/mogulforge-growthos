# GrowthOS soft-onboarding demo: walkthrough for Adam

**When:** Saturday, October 17, 2026
**Who:** Climate Simulation Solutions (HVAC) and Painless Roofing
**How long:** 10 to 12 minutes. About 5 minutes per company, plus questions.

Everything on screen is made-up practice data. Every customer name ends in
"(TEST)", every company name starts with "TEST Demo", phone numbers are 555
numbers and emails are @example.com. No texts or emails go out from this demo.

---

## Before you start (5 minutes before the call)

1. Open GrowthOS and sign in as the demo owner for the company you are showing.
   You will see **TEST Demo - Climate Simulation Solutions (HVAC)** or
   **TEST Demo - Painless Roofing** at the top left.
2. Click **Today**. The date should read **Saturday, October 17**. If it shows a
   different day, use the arrows next to the date to get to October 17.
3. Keep a second tab open on **Customers & Jobs** so you can jump there quickly.

If you are showing both companies on one call, show the one you know best first.

## If asked

**Is this live today?**
What you saw runs on our test system with made-up customers. Tomorrow we can
start setting up your company, your team logins, and import your customer list.
Your real website form and day-to-day use go live after the cutover, when we
switch from the test system to your live account.

**Will it text or email my customers?**
Not in this demo. Texts and emails are switched off (held) so nothing goes out
by accident. When you're live, messages only go out after you connect your
own business email and phone number and approve the wording.

**Can I bring my Housecall Pro customers over?**
Yes, customers come over from a Housecall Pro export. Old jobs, estimates and
invoice history from Housecall Pro do not come over automatically yet; we bring
those in separately where it matters.

**Can customers pay online?**
In this demo, payments are recorded by your staff (check, cash, or card taken
another way). Online card payments are a separate step after go-live.

**Does it work on my phone?**
Yes, your techs use it in the phone's web browser. Push alerts to phones are
set up per device once you're live.

**What does it cost?** Point them to the pricing you've already agreed to share;
nothing in this demo is billed.

## Operator notes (for whoever loads the practice data)

- The practice data is pinned to **Saturday, October 17, 2026** (company time
  zone America/New_York). Re-running the loader never moves an existing visit
  to another day. Edits or uncertain custody produce **HOLD**; the loader never
  overwrites the edit or silently creates a replacement. A failed first attempt
  may already have acknowledged effects, so retain its journal and receipt.
- Local rehearsal: start `node scripts/demo/demo-runtime.mjs`, then pass the
  manifest path it prints to
  `node scripts/demo/seed-soft-onboarding.mjs --base-url http://127.0.0.1:<port> --local-manifest <path> [--apply] [--verify]`.
- Staging needs a ROOT-reviewed `--execution-manifest` and its exact raw
  `--execution-manifest-sha`, binding the company/site/owner/staff IDs to actual
  catalog/database, immutable image, frontend, deployed source, config, holds
  and absolute runtime-stop proof. Proof expires within five minutes. This
  source refuses any staging admission past **22:00Z October 16**; a prepared or
  partial extension cannot extend that deadline. It creates no companies/users.
- A run only counts as done when it ends **APPLIED** with read-back PASS and,
  with `--verify`, every expected name visible on every page. Any other ending
  (BLOCKED, HOLD, INCOMPLETE, UNKNOWN) exits nonzero: stop and ask before the
  call rather than demoing partial data.
- Each run writes its own folder under
  `C:/Forge/artifacts/growthos-demo-seed-correction-20261016/runs/` (receipt,
  screenshots, hash list). Permanent mutation custody uses a fixed sibling
  `custody/` namespace independent of report/lock directory and run ID. Never
  delete/reset those reservations or retry UNKNOWN. A completed fixture can
  only be verified with the same retained IDs; edits require reconciliation.
- [Execution guard contract](SEED-EXECUTION-GUARDS.md) specifies proof fields,
  retained resource identities, irreversible-attempt limits and reconciliation
  responsibility. These source checks do not establish hosted or launch proof.
