## Delivered bounded scope

Three coordinated lanes add forward-only owner-approved standing policies,
signed bounded keypad intake after the staff bridge, caller-originated one-call
SMS grants and an accountable office worklist. Explicit identity review and an
exact customer-agreed time can produce a native capacity/conflict-checked visit,
including repeat customers without a fabricated acquisition opportunity.

The original call, handoff and callback task remain canonical. The fixed
`missed-call-care:<callId>:sms:v1` response identity spans old and new transports.
Transactionally owned acknowledgment plus execution precedes the physical
no-retry/no-redirect POST; final checks cover current policy, both owners,
approver, call/task/binding/recipient, grant, holds, suppression, other inbound
activity, expiration, hours and shared company/phone allowances. Unknown outcomes
stay reserved; late provider evidence cannot grant another send.

Admission occurs only with insertion of the original new signed inbound call.
Old callbacks cannot adopt a later policy. Exact callback replays return encrypted
saved responses; changed duplicates stop. The optional disclosure is outside
Gather so early keypad input cannot skip it. The typed inbound code is not kept
in the legacy SMS body, and it grants no general consent or automatic enrollment.
No full conversation, recording/transcription, AI model call, autonomous booking,
emergency dispatch, warranty decision, price quote or real send is delivered here.

Eight human-session, tenant/role-bound routes have OpenAPI and generated client/
Zod contracts. Office mutations use lifecycle/schedule/intake locks, current
canonical facts and retained request identities; no network work runs inside
their transaction. Scheduler integration isolates stage failures and bounds
rotating work. Office response deadlines are independent of acknowledgment
deferral. Disabled policies and closed/reassigned office work are respected.
