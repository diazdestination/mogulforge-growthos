# Customer work record — operator guide

1. Open **Customers & Jobs → Customers** and choose **Customer record** on the
   intended contact. Check the customer name, contact details and company first.
   Matching names or shared addresses do not merge customers.
2. Choose **Visits**, **Service & equipment**, **Properties**, **Opportunities**,
   **Jobs**, **Estimates**, **Invoices**, **Tasks** or **History**. Invoice visibility
   follows your current role. Only the selected section is loaded.
3. Use **Older records** / **Newer page** for history. Pages are newest-created
   first, not appointment-date order. Refresh restarts at the newest page.
4. **Open visit** opens that exact appointment even outside the schedule range.
   To work on it, choose **Show this day in schedule**, then the existing record's
   edit control. The summary deliberately excludes private notes and is read-only.
5. **Open service** returns to the existing Service Desk. Its reminder/booking
   approvals, actual agreements, send restrictions and uncertainty handling still
   apply. A recorded coverage date is not a warranty eligibility determination.
6. To add a service date, select the customer and deliberately choose the property.
   Leave **No property linked** if it is not known. Load more properties if needed.
   Changing the customer clears the property. A failed lookup must be retried;
   it does not prove there are no properties. Existing histories are not relinked.

Jobs/estimates open their related opportunity, not a guessed old record. Invoices
and tasks open their authoritative workflows. A property-directory link is labeled
as all customers; it is not an exact property editor. Safe History events do not
prove message delivery, customer confirmation, completed work or collected money.

Customer/service records can exist with no opportunity. Do not create a fake lead
to make history visible. Conflicting parent links and invalid/overlong service
ancestry are withheld; investigate the original records rather than merging or
rewriting history. These pages are not a full data export.

If the link names a different company, sign in to the matching company; the link
never switches accounts. If current access cannot be verified, reload/sign in
again. Records are not promised offline. Provider activation, real installed-phone
notification proof and both contractors' workday acceptance remain separate.
