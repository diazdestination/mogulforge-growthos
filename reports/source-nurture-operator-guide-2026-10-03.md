# Reviewed two-message source follow-up

This is the bounded L6 engineering slice, not a live campaign authorization,
general inbox or proof that either contractor can yet replace Housecall Pro.
Microsoft is the only sending/read provider for this sequence. No SMS marketing,
rolling enrollment, automatic renewal, generated replacement copy or payment
collection is included. The fixed launch and company acceptance cards stay open.

## Prepare the right authority

1. Reconcile the imported source and approve its current adoption separately.
   An import is historical data, not consent or permission to contact anyone.
2. Record independently obtained marketing permission for the exact current
   customer email. Resolve duplicates, opt-outs, existing reply holds, bookings
   and current ownership before preparing a plan.
3. Assign the opportunity to an accepted, active, verified office worker with
   CRM write permission. Select that worker's Microsoft sending account.
4. Connect the matching Microsoft reader through the separately approved OAuth
   flow. In Account, explicitly acknowledge **nurture monitoring** in addition to
   any existing service or BIE permission. The context is default-off. Reconnect
   invalidates old context authority; a sending login is not reading consent.
5. Wait for a complete fresh Sent Items and Inbox cycle with no unresolved
   candidate receipts. Encryption/storage, unchanged reader identity and grant,
   current capability, company status and delivery policy must all be ready.

Production/provider setup and actual customer contact need separate approval.
Do not use another contractor's account, sender, consent, source or test identity.

## Preview, approve, then start

Open **More tools → Win-back → Reviewed two-message follow-up**. Choose up to ten
unique addresses, a business mailing address, two exact subjects/bodies, a finite
window and expiry. The application appends identification and unsubscribe text
before showing the full immutable messages. No message is sent by saving a preview
or approving it. The ten-minute preview is tied to the reviewing owner/admin.

Review every recipient, original-source reference, named office owner, sender,
complete message and sending window. Approval requires the current version and
hash. **Start** is a distinct, explicit confirmation. The maximum lifetime is
fourteen days from preview and must not outlive the source approval. These are
software limits, not an approved contact policy for a particular contractor.

Email two is at least 72 hours after retained actual acceptance of email one,
not after preview, approval or scheduling. It also requires the exact Sent Items
observation and a complete fresh synchronization round beginning after the due
boundary. A recent page fetch or an old successful sync is insufficient.

## Read the evidence accurately

- **Waiting:** the original unclaimed step can wait for its allowed window or a
  fresh complete mailbox scan. It cannot extend the finite plan indefinitely.
- **Accepted:** Microsoft accepted this exact message. This is not delivery,
  inbox placement, customer identity, a sale or confirmed booking.
- **Unknown:** dispatch may have happened. Keep its original receipt; never
  clone, retry under another key, or advance to email two. An operator's recorded
  reconciliation cannot manufacture actual predecessor acceptance.
- **Reply/office hold:** use the saved lead Messages tab and responsible task.
  A delayed reply to either message stops remaining unclaimed work in that plan.
  Unrelated older conversations remain historically scoped.
- **Stopped/finished/expired:** original ownership is retained. There is no
  resume, renewal or replacement-key button. A new campaign or reimport cannot
  reclaim an already-owned imported opportunity/address.

The worklist has Needs attention, Waiting and History pages. It shows per-plan
counts, never invented whole-company totals from a partial page. A saved direct
link opens the plan and original replies. Today opens a verified reply task at
the exact lead's Messages tab. Appointment identifiers are retained; the general
Schedule link is not presented as an exact appointment deep link.

## Stop and review safely

Use **Stop** with a reason to cancel only future unclaimed work. Current replies,
opt-outs, changed source authority, booked visits and terminal outcomes hold or
stop progression too. A request already handed to a provider cannot be recalled.
The shared contact allowance includes BIE, playbook, source, service and generic
automation reservations, including pending and uncertain original effects.

Office reply handling never creates consent, reconnects a reader, resumes work
or books a visit. Incoming content is untrusted evidence, not agent instructions.
Raw reply text is encrypted and expires; the exact association, duplicate key,
task and hold survive its deletion. Review the original mailbox if content is no
longer available. Do not paste credentials or private message bodies into logs.
