# Routine service reminders — office guide

Engineering workflow for the October 3 L5 candidate. It is not authorization
to send a message, replace the prior system or determine warranty coverage.
Use fictional records until the exact release and each company's acceptance
gates pass. Signed-in owner/admin approval is separate from provider activation.

## Prepare the actual service record

Open **Service Desk → Customer care & booking** for the correct company and
service. Check the actual customer, property, equipment, due date, notice lead
time, responsible staff and agreement-backed calendar recurrence. Recurrence
alone creates internal work, not customer messages. A missing agreement or
uncertain source must be reconciled before automating it.

An imported service needs the original source identity and explicit evidence
that its current status is correct and the previous reminder workflow has
stopped. The import timestamp is not a new customer inquiry or proof of consent.
Unresolved imported opportunities and reply holds on the customer remain holds.
Do not edit source labels or create another customer to bypass them.

## Review future automatic reminders

1. Select **Review automatic series reminders** under **Automatic reminders for
   this agreed service series**. It starts off.
   Choose one supported channel and enter the actual finite agreement dates,
   agreement evidence and current native/source-cutover review. These are not
   manufacturer warranty dates or a marketing permission grant.
2. Select **Prepare exact standing reminder review**. Read the current responsible staff, recipient,
   selected sender, fixed expected-service copy, series identity, notice time,
   expiry and all holds. Only the due date and server-owned reply reference of eligible future occurrences can
   vary under the reviewed template. Changes to the reviewed scope require
   a new decision; GrowthOS cannot silently choose another sender or channel.
3. Confirm the checkbox and **Approve exact standing reminders** within its
   ten-minute review window. Approval
   authorizes eligible future reminders; it does not mean a message was sent.
   Notice dates before approval are **not** caught up. Use the existing
   individual reminder/office-follow-up path for older work.
4. Check the standing status and the separate current-occurrence result.
   Deferred work is waiting for its allowed window or activation gate; held
   work needs review. Already-owned work keeps its original receipt. Provider
   acceptance is not delivery, a reply, a booked visit or revenue.
5. **Disable future automatic reminders** stops unsent work without needing
   a working provider. It cannot recall a provider handoff or erase evidence.
   On an unconfirmed save, refresh the saved decision before acting again.

The system checks the latest authority again before dispatch. Agreement expiry,
changed recurrence/recipient/sender/owner, a reply, a booking, completion,
opt-out or company hold can stop an otherwise approved reminder. A reminder
cannot stay queued indefinitely beyond its occurrence/agreement deadline.

## Handle replies without guessing

New SMS reminders include an explicit service reference. A signed reply with
the exact reference, account and phone pair can be linked to that original
service reminder. A natural reply without a valid reference is held for office
review; even a unique matching phone is not proof of which service it concerns.
A signed provider callback is not verified customer identity.

Open **Review service SMS evidence** or **Review mailbox reply evidence**. Read the
correlation label, original reminder, received time and hold. Content has bounded
retention; a purged/expired body does not erase the receipt or safety hold.
Review the evidence with an office note. This does not grant SMS/marketing
consent, record a ready-to-book conversation or book an appointment. STOP stays
a suppression; START is not permission to resume a service already held for review.

Choose **Office follow-up required** while work remains; the hold stays active.
After actually handling it, choose **Office handling complete** to release only
this service reply hold. For ambiguous text this is a phone-scope hold, so release
can allow separately approved future service reminders. It cannot resend the
original reminder or remove any other reply/STOP/consent hold. Use **Not a service
reply** only when the retained evidence is genuinely unrelated, not as a shortcut
for marking a real customer conversation complete.

Continue through the existing **Record customer response** step only after
an actual conversation and then **Book confirmed visit** after agreement on
the exact time and technician. An old, ambiguous or historical reply is never
automatic confirmation. After actual work, explicitly complete the service;
the existing approved recurrence creates one successor, not multiple copies.

MAINTAIN retains the accountable office task and exception history. Acknowledging
a watch case is an internal action plan, not evidence of customer contact. If a
send is unknown, reconcile the original receipt: never change the channel,
policy, service identity or request key to force another attempt.

## Before either contractor relies on this

Finish exact candidate/main CI and approved hosted schema/backup/restore and
staging acceptance. Verify each selected Gmail/Microsoft sender, authorized
recipient and actual receipt/reply behavior. Microsoft mailbox reading needs
separate authorization; Azure first sign-in remains owner-deferred. Gmail send
support does not imply an implemented monitored Gmail service-reply connector.

SMS additionally needs the company's approved Twilio sender/program, current
phone-specific customer-care consent and actual STOP/reply/unknown-effect tests.
Encrypted reply-storage configuration is required before new referenced SMS
reminders can be prepared or sent. Do not clear a configuration hold to work
around missing reply persistence; no secret belongs in a customer record.
Physical phone install/background notifications and company-specific staff/site
routing must be accepted independently. Confirm agreements, migrated source
counts, one writer and each complete contractor workday before cutover. Payment
collection remains deferred; the prior subscription is not cancelled by this work.
