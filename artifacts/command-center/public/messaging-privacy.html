Messaging privacy

Prelaunch draft · September 27, 2026. Text delivery is not activated. The legal operator, business address and full product privacy policy require confirmation before launch or registration.

This notice describes the optional MogulForge text program and requested business calls prepared for GrowthOS. It does not cover a client business’s separate messaging program.

Information used

We store your phone number, selected message purposes, disclosure version, confirmation and withdrawal dates, and consent source. A confirmation code is stored as a hash while enrollment is pending. Ordinary incoming text replies may be stored in your MogulForge customer record so staff can respond. Delivery records include provider message identifiers, dates, status and error codes. Call records contain telephone numbers, dates, direction, status and the staff authorization for a requested call. Call recording is off.

How information is shared

Twilio and telecommunications carriers process the phone numbers, message content and routing metadata needed to deliver texts and calls. Authorized MogulForge staff can access relevant customer communications. Mobile information and text opt-in consent will not be sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process it only as necessary to operate this program.

Your choices

Choose purposes independently at Text preferences. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to withdraw from texts. START alone does not restore program consent; confirm your choices again. Reply HELP or contact Adam@mogulforge.ai for assistance, corrections or a privacy request.

Retention and security

The prepared application retains consent and delivery records with the customer account; no automatic retention period has yet been approved. A minimal suppression record is retained to prevent future unwanted messages. Access is restricted by organization, credentials are kept on the server, and provider callbacks are authenticated. The final retention schedule and deletion process must be published before activation.

Client businesses

Future client-branded programs need their own verified business information, sender configuration, consent and policies. Painless Roofing and other prospective clients are not included in this program.
