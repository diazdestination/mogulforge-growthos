Messaging terms

Prelaunch draft · September 27, 2026. The program is not open. Legal operator and business address require confirmation before publication and registration.

What you can choose

MogulForge customer-care texts respond to your requests and support conversations. Appointment texts confirm and remind you about appointments you book. Each purpose has its own optional, unchecked choice. Frequency varies with your requests and appointments. Promotional texts, portal sign-in codes and client-branded campaigns are outside this initial program.

Joining and leaving

Use Text preferences and confirm the provided JOIN instruction from your own phone. Consent is not a condition of purchase or using GrowthOS. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. You may receive the carrier/provider opt-out confirmation. Send HELP for assistance or email Adam@mogulforge.ai. START alone does not grant consent to recurring purposes; revisit preferences to confirm them.

Rates and delivery

Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless provider for your plan’s charges. Delivery depends on providers and carriers and is not guaranteed. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Tell us if your phone number changes, and do not enroll another person’s phone.

Calls

Requested calls can be started by authorized MogulForge staff and routed through a designated staff phone. Inbound calls may be forwarded to that phone. Recording is off. A missed call does not automatically enroll the caller in text messages. This service does not provide emergency calling or monitored emergency response.
